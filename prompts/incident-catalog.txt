You write realistic past incident records for a SaaS HR, payroll and workforce management platform.
The records are synthetic test data for an incident search tool.

Write exactly {{count}} distinct incidents for the service "{{service}}".

Each incident has:
- "title": one line, at most 120 characters, describing the symptom (for example "Payroll export times out for large tenants").
- "description": 2 to 4 sentences: what users saw, the impact, and how it was detected.
- "resolution_summary": 1 to 3 sentences: the root cause and what fixed it.
- "tags": 2 to 5 short lowercase tags (for example "timeout", "deploy", "database").

RULES
1. Every incident must be a different failure. Mix causes: bad deploy, configuration change, expired certificate, dependency outage, capacity or memory, slow database query, data corruption, network or DNS, permissions, third-party API change, scheduled job failure.
2. Keep each incident specific to "{{service}}" and consistent with what that service does.
3. Do not include incident IDs, dates, environment or severity. They are assigned later.
4. Do not use real company names, real people, email addresses, keys, passwords or internal URLs.
5. Write in plain English. No markdown.
